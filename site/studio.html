Owner only. Publish the $PRYSM contract address, buy back and burn on the Pons curve, burn supply the burn wallet holds, and check the public record before it goes live.

Studio keystored in this tab only

Contract address

The $PRYSM token on Robinhood Chain. It must be a Pons token (the factory is checked). Once set, the landing shows the burn record and /api/token returns it.

Burn walletbuys and burns

Use a separate, fresh wallet that only holds the ETH (and $PRYSM) you plan to burn. Never paste the dev or treasury wallet. The key is encrypted on the server with PRYSM_SECRET and is only ever used to buy $PRYSM on its Pons curve with the dead address as recipient, or to send $PRYSM it holds to the dead address.

Buyback & burn

Buys $PRYSM on its Pons curve with ETH from the burn wallet, the dead address as recipient. Every token bought is burned in the same transaction.

Supply burnno buy

Send the $PRYSM you want to burn to the burn wallet first, then enter dollars at the curve price or press Max to burn all of it. One transfer to the dead address.

Burn record previewwhat the landing shows

The same panel as the landing, read from the chain. Leave the box empty for the published $PRYSM, or paste any Robinhood Chain token to preview its burns first.
